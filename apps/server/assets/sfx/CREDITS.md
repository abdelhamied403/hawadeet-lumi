# Sound effect credits

All effects here are public domain (CC0) and need no credit:
- birds: "Bird chirping sounds", OpenGameArt.org (https://opengameart.org/content/bird-chirping-sounds)
- giggle: "Group giggling" by AuraVoice, OpenGameArt.org (https://opengameart.org/content/group-giggling)
- meow: "Meow" by IgnasD, OpenGameArt.org (https://opengameart.org/content/meow)
- pop (plop_01), splash (splash_01), bell (bell_01): "100 CC0 SFX", OpenGameArt.org (https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx)
- footsteps (sped up) and knock: CC0 recordings from OpenGameArt.org, also used by the horror series
- whoosh and sparkle are synthesized by this project with ffmpeg

The horror series' effects are in horror/, with their own CREDITS.md (one of them needs a credit, which the
pipeline adds to descriptions).
